 ## Overview

A government Single Sign-On (SSO) system allows people to use one account to access different government services. To keep this system safe, six technology areas work together. Software uses OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect to help users log in securely and confirm their identity. Cybersecurity uses FIDO2 and passkeys to protect users from fake websites and phishing attacks. Mobile security uses Android permissions to control what an app can access on a phone. Networking uses DNS over HTTPS (DoH) to encrypt DNS requests and make it harder for others on the network to see them. Artificial intelligence (AI) helps detect unusual login activity that may be a sign of an attack. Finally, Oman's Personal Data Protection Law helps ensure that citizens' personal information is handled safely and responsibly. Together, these six areas help protect user accounts, keep personal data private, and make government online services safer and more trustworthy.
